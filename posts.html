The Indigenous Language Innovation Week Hackathon

In May 2026 I took part in the Indigenous Language Innovation Week, hosted by the Isabelo Sempumelelo Foundation and Singawe Innovative at DUT. It started with a virtual bootcamp and closed with a two-day hackathon focused on using technology to advance and promote South Africa's indigenous languages. My team didn't place in the top 3, but the experience taught me more about real-world tech than the result did — working under pressure with a team, turning an idea into something workable in two days, and seeing how technology can genuinely serve a social cause. It's one of the experiences that pushed me further into pursuing ICT seriously.
